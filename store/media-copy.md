## Apple rules applied

- **App Review Guideline 2.3.4 (app previews):** the preview shows only screen captures of the app itself. No device frame, hands,
  home screen, Photos app or iOS share sheet (beat 4 starts on Nouveau's own share-intent screen), no closing wordmark card, no call
  to action and no text pointing outside the app (no URL, store name or "download"). Narration, captions and label pills are allowed.
  Vendor names (backend, payments and speech providers) never appear on screen; sign-in provider rows are off-frame. Premium, the
  paywall, guest mode, proofs and email/WhatsApp ingestion are not shown (not live or internal).
- **Creative assets and screenshots:** no prices, URLs, awards or other platforms in any asset. Text uses the system font and the
  semantic colours; the accent is only a tint.
- **Specs:** iPhone 6.9" screenshots 1320x2868 PNG without alpha; Header 3840x1646 (21:9) and Search Results 3000x2000 (3:2), PNG
  without alpha, with the text inside the safe areas (Header: the middle 65% of the width; Search Results: the middle 70% and 150 px
  top and bottom); app preview 886x1920 at 30 fps, H.264 with stereo AAC, 15-30 s, light mode, status bar 9:41.
- Upload and the poster frame (about 2.4 s, the Transactions screen with the category bar) are chosen by hand in App Store Connect.
